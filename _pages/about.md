**Dr. Ujjal Mandal** is a researcher whose Ph.D. degree was awarded on **12 September 2026**. He pursued his doctoral research at the **[Indian Institute of Information Technology Allahabad (IIIT Allahabad)](https://www.iiita.ac.in/)** from 19 August 2021 to 29 August 2026, under the supervision of **[Prof. Ratan K. Saha](https://www.iiita.ac.in/faculty)**.

His primary research focused on developing **two-dimensional (2D) Helmholtz equation solvers for biomedical ultrasonics and photoacoustics**, with particular emphasis on numerical modeling and computational methods for wave propagation in realistic media.

To implement and accelerate his computational research, he utilized programming languages and technologies such as **C/C++, MATLAB, and CUDA**. His work also involved modern high-performance computing (HPC), parallel computing, and distributed computing techniques. He gained access to advanced computational resources through IIIT Allahabad, IIT (BHU) Varanasi, and other institutions providing HPC facilities.

His research contributions include publications in leading scientific journals, including the *Journal of the Acoustical Society of America (JASA)* and *Photoacoustics*.

Dr. Mandal has also collaborated with researchers from reputed national and international institutions. His research network includes **[Prof. Ben T. Cox of University College London (UCL)](https://profiles.ucl.ac.uk/5280-ben-cox)**, **[Prof. Jagpreet Singh of IIT Ropar](https://iitrpr.ac.in/department/cse/faculty)**, and researchers at IIIT Allahabad. These collaborations reflect his engagement with the broader scientific community in computational acoustics, biomedical ultrasonics, photoacoustic imaging, and numerical wave modeling.
